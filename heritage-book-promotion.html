A reader-first mindset

Every book has a story worth sharing.

Heritage Book Promotion is built around a simple idea: authors deserve thoughtful communication and promotion that respects their work. We aim to help you present your book clearly, find suitable ways to talk about it, and make informed decisions about your next steps.

  • Plans discussed around your book, genre, and goals
  • Clear expectations before any work begins
  • Promotional ideas focused on relevant readers
  • Honest communication about what promotion can and cannot guarantee
Our promise: promotion can support visibility, but no ethical service can guarantee bestseller status, sales, reviews, or a specific number of readers.
